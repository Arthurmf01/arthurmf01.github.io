Build · INSEAD Gen AI course project

Reel Reads

A swipe feed where every card is a 20-second idea from a book. The next card is chosen from how you swipe.

Stack
HTML mockup · AI-generated author videos with captions · adaptive recommender design
Status
Specification, seed catalogue and a working mockup with two video cards
Code
github.com/Arthurmf01/reel-reads

Why it exists

MBA reading lists are long and most of us never finish them. People already learn in short videos; the format just rarely carries the good ideas. Reel Reads tests whether one idea per card, from a book worth reading, holds attention the way a feed does.

How it works

  1. A seed catalogue of books recommended in MBA courses.
  2. Each card is one idea in about 20 seconds: a short author-style video, synced captions, and three takeaways at the end.
  3. The feed learns from swipes. Skip fast and it moves on; watch to the end and it goes deeper on that book or theme.

What I learned

The model is the easy part; the recommender is the product. And synthetic video raises real questions of consent: the cards only use likenesses we have the right to use.

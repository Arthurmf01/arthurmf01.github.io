AI builds

The agents that run my day.

Voice notes, meetings, emails, links and job boards all land in one memory. Agents sort them, draft the next step and wait for my approval. I built them to be tested, traced and kept on a budget, the same questions a company asks before it trusts AI with real work.

Around all of it

  • Nothing sends without my yes. Every outgoing message waits for approval on my phone.
  • A critic reads first. A separate agent plays the recipient and tries to tear each draft apart.
  • Tested before it changes. Ten real past captures are replayed and graded after every change to the agent's instructions.
  • Checked every morning. Each scheduled job's last run, its error log and how its secrets are stored.

London, covering Africa

Africa was a no-sell zone.

I joined while MuleSoft was still being absorbed into Salesforce, chose to stay with the MuleSoft business unit, and was handed Africa. No accounts, no playbook, no local team. I was 25, covering 52 countries of a continent from London, and fairly sure someone would work out I was improvising.

By the time I left, it was close to $10M in annual recurring revenue.

Enterprise only: sales cycles of six to eighteen months, a few large deals a year, not volume. On each one I orchestrated a cross-functional team: solution engineers, business development reps, partner managers and value services consultants.

  • 0customers on day one
  • 14countries with customers
  • 7-figureannual contract, the largest, with a leading Angolan bank
